# Market Outlook

Market Outlook is a market research and portfolio dashboard in development for stocks and cryptocurrencies. It is designed to bring portfolio tracking, watchlists, price charts, and financial news together with AI-assisted analysis of the narratives surrounding individual assets.

## End Goal

Create a single place to track investments and understand what is driving the assets that matter to you. Users will be able to explore current and historical prices, review relevant news, and generate a market outlook grounded in recent evidence, including the current narrative, a bull case, and a bear case.

The finished application will combine a clear, responsive interface with useful financial context, helping users connect market developments to the assets they own or follow.
